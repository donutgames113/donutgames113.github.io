About Henry Matthew Hutchinson

Henry Matthew Hutchinson, also known online as Donut or donutgames113, is a multi-award-winning game developer and graphics designer. His work spans game development, Roblox experiences, game systems, Lua scripting, graphics design, visual design, branding, user interfaces, and web projects.

This page is the central profile for information about Henry Matthew Hutchinson and his work. It links to his public developer profiles and portfolio so that people and search engines can connect his name with his projects.

Game development

Henry develops interactive game experiences, with a focus on Roblox development, gameplay systems, Lua scripting, multiplayer features, and player-facing interfaces.

Graphics design

Henry's graphics design work includes visual identity, branding, promotional graphics, game-related visuals, and digital presentation. His development and design work combines the technical side of interactive experiences with their visual communication.
